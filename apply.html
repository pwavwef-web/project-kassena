Help us build Kasem AI.

Project Kasena is partnering with Indigen World on an ambitious 18-month mission to build the foundational digital infrastructure for the Kasem language. We are creating a community-driven digital dictionary and translation web app that will eventually power a full Kasem AI assistant capable of text and voice recognition.

We are actively recruiting a core MVP team to launch our pilot in Northern Ghana. Whether you are a student, a tech professional, or a respected community elder — we have a role for you.
