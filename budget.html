Executive summary

Why this seed grant matters now

Project Kasena is a technology-driven initiative to preserve and digitise the Kasem language spoken by the Kasena-Nankana people of Ghana’s Upper East Region. The Phase 1 MVP is intentionally lean: it focuses on collecting, validating, and organising language data before moving to larger AI products.

  • ✓
    Phase 1 goal: Digitise and validate 20,000 conversational phrases and vocabulary entries.
  • ✓
    Structured incentives: Reward high-quality youth contributions only after expert review and approval.
  • ✓
    Long-term value: Build the evidence base required for translation tools, voice interfaces, and school-ready learning products.

Project framing

“Every language lost is a world gone silent. Project Kasena is a community’s act of remembrance — and a nation’s investment in its own future.”

Funding framework

Four investment pillars with clear payout logic and measurable outputs

Pillar 1 — Youth Contributors

GHS 10,000 · 40% allocated to bounty payments at GHS 0.50 per verified phrase.

This turns language preservation into a meaningful income opportunity while encouraging wide participation across SHS and university networks.

Pillar 2 — Elder & Teacher Validators

GHS 8,000 · 32% funds 3–4 validators through structured stipends, preserving quality, dialect sensitivity, and cultural meaning.

The plan assumes up to 4 validators receiving GHS 1,000 per month for 2 months.

Pillar 3 — Community Mobilisation & Data Drives

GHS 4,000 · 16% supports organised school activations, radio outreach, travel logistics, print materials, and visible project branding.

Target venues include Navrongo SHS, Notre Dame Girls’ SHS, CKT-UTAS Navrongo Campus, and community centres in Paga, Chuchuliga, and Sandema.

Pillar 4 — Tech Operations & Contingency

GHS 3,000 · 12% covers domain setup, OTP verification, early infrastructure upgrades, and a contingency reserve for emerging needs.

This keeps the MVP resilient without overbuilding expensive infrastructure too early.

Technology operations breakdown

Domain registration: GHS 150 for kasena-ai.org or kasena.app.

SMS gateway / OTP: GHS 400 for trusted mobile verification and duplicate-account control.

Contingency reserve: GHS 1,950 for unforeseen delays, transport needs, or partnership opportunities.

Why the bounty model works

  • 1
    It makes preservation economically meaningful for students and drives volume quickly.
  • 2
    Payments are tied to validation, so quality remains central.
  • 3
    Leaderboards and public recognition strengthen organic participation.

Why validators are non-negotiable

  • 1
    Kasem is largely oral, so raw internet data cannot be trusted as a formal corpus.
  • 2
    Expert review protects proverb meaning, idioms, and dialect-sensitive usage.
  • 3
    Validation raises credibility with universities, research partners, and future funders.

Operational safeguards

  • 1
    OTP verification reduces duplicate accounts and payout abuse.
  • 2
    Free-tier infrastructure keeps overhead low during early validation.
  • 3
    On-platform reporting supports transparency for public funding partners.
