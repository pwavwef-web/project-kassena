2. Additional Resources & Long-Term Funding Strategy

The $500 Aspire Seed Grant is the catalyst to launch the MVP and validate the crowdsourcing model. The total estimated cost for completing Phase 1 (digitizing 20,000 validated phrases) is approximately 25,000 GHS.

  • Strategic Community Capital (Remaining 20,000 GHS)

    After early MVP traction and validation milestones in Week 9, Project Kasena will pitch local stakeholders, including Members of Parliament (Navrongo Central, Chiana-Paga) and District Assemblies, to secure Common Fund grants and scale community data bounty payouts. A community donation channel will also support diaspora contributions.

  • In-Kind Resources (Labor & Mentorship)

    A task force of 5-7 university student volunteers (UCC and CKT-UTAS) supports frontend development, marketing, and data organization. The project also leverages mentorship from the University of Cape Coast network and local school administrators for low-cost access to student contributors.

  • Existing Open-Source Infrastructure

    To minimize costs, the MVP is built on open-source frameworks (React/Next.js) and initially relies on free cloud service tiers (Vercel, Supabase) before paid scaling becomes necessary.
