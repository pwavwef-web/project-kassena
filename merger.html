Strategic Update

Project Kasena is now part of Indigen World.

Following our highly successful 90-day joint pilot, Project Kasena's mission, community, and technology are being fully absorbed into Indigen World to scale indigenous language preservation globally.

A massive leap forward for Kasem AI.

What started as a targeted local initiative to build a digital dictionary for the Kasena-Nankana people has evolved. Our custom-built linguistic infrastructure—powered by Next.js and Python—will now serve as the foundational "engine" for Indigen World's global cultural platform.

By merging under the Indigen World umbrella, we guarantee long-term sustainability, better financial backing for our contributors, and a much wider reach for our heritage.

Contributor FAQ

Questions about the transition?

Here is what you need to know about your account, your data, and your pending rewards.

What happens to my pending points and wallet balance?

Your wallet is 100% safe. All existing points, both pending and approved, are being migrated seamlessly to the new Indigen World system. Your withdrawal thresholds remain exactly the same.

Do I need to re-apply for my role?

No. If you are an active Contributor, Validator, or Community Captain, your role automatically carries over to the new combined organization. Our leadership team remains actively involved.

Will the Project Kasena website disappear?

For the immediate future, this portal will remain active for data submission and wallet management. Eventually, all interfaces will redirect to a dedicated Kasem portal within the Indigen World application.

Who owns the language data now?

The Kasena-Nankana community remains the ultimate custodian of the data. Indigen World acts as the global steward, ensuring the open-source dictionary and educational tools remain free for our people forever.
