Contributor payment system

Earn rewards for verified phrases.

Project Kasena uses a transparent Points → Wallet → Withdrawal model. Every verified contribution earns points that accumulate in your digital wallet. Once you reach the minimum threshold, withdraw your earnings through mobile money.

No points are awarded until contributions pass validator review. Quality always comes first.

Points structure

How much each verified contribution earns.

Points are awarded only after your submission passes validator review. The more valuable the contribution to the Kasem corpus, the higher the reward.

10pts

Approved Translation

English-to-Kasem or Kasem-to-English word and phrase translations that pass validator review for accuracy and context.

5pts

Approved Example Sentence

Original Kasem sentences demonstrating natural usage, grammar, and conversational context for language learners and AI training.

20pts

Cultural Expression / Proverb

Verified proverbs, idioms, and cultural expressions that capture the richness of Kasem heritage and oral tradition.

★pts

Sponsored Data Campaigns

Special collection drives with variable point values. These campaigns target specific domains like agriculture, health, or education vocabulary.

Withdrawal policy

Minimum thresholds for cashing out.

A minimum withdrawal keeps operational costs low and maximizes the value of each transaction for contributors. As you build trust, your minimum threshold drops.

New Contributors

GHS 20

Equivalent to 1,000 points. The starting threshold for all new contributors joining the platform.

Trusted Contributors

GHS 10

Reduced threshold for contributors with a proven track record of high-quality, validated submissions.

Validators

GHS 50

Higher threshold for elder and teacher validators who receive structured monthly stipends for their review work.

Integrity controls

Built-in protections for fair rewards.

Every safeguard exists to protect the quality of the Kasem language dataset and ensure that rewards flow to genuine, high-quality contributors.

  • 📱
    Phone number verification Every account is tied to a verified mobile number to prevent duplicate sign-ups and ensure payout delivery.
  • 🔄
    Duplicate content detection Automated screening flags repeated or copied submissions before they reach human validators.
  • 👁️
    Validator approval No points are awarded until an elder or teacher validator confirms accuracy, dialect purity, and cultural meaning.
  • 📅
    Account age checks New accounts may face adjusted thresholds or delayed payouts to prevent rush abuse during onboarding.
  • 🚨
    Suspicious activity monitoring Patterns like rapid-fire submissions, copy-paste behaviour, or unusual submission times trigger manual review.
  • 📄
    Full audit logs Every transaction, point credit, and withdrawal is recorded and exportable for transparency and accountability.
